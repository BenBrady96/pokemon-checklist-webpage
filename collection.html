Help & shortcuts

Marking cards

  • Tap a card to mark it owned, and tap again to unmark it.
  • Mark many at once: on a phone, press and hold a card, then drag across others. With a mouse, click and drag. Hold near the top or bottom edge to keep scrolling.
  • Shift-click marks every card between your last click and this one.
  • Count mode () makes each tap add a copy, which is handy when logging packs. Use − to remove one.
  • Quick add takes card numbers such as 1-22, 55x2.
  • Card info () shows where to get a card, rough pull odds and its market price, with links to check live prices.
  • Every change can be undone from the pop-up or with Ctrl+Z.

Keeping your collection safe

  • Your collection is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Use Export backup now and then to copy a sync code or save a file. It covers every set you’ve started. Import backup on another device takes the code, a sync link or the file.
  • On iPhone and iPad, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Safari can clear saved data for websites you haven’t visited in a while, but not for apps on your Home Screen, and the app also works offline. The app keeps its own collection, separate from Safari’s, so use Import backup to move your cards into it.
