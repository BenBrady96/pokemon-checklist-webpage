Privacy & cookie policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version: this site doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t track you and doesn’t ask for any personal information. Visits are counted without cookies and without identifying you. Your checklist is saved on your own device, and nobody else can see it unless you share a link.

Who we are

Binder Tracker (“the checklist”, “we”, “us”) is a free, unofficial, fan-made website for keeping track of cards from trading card game (TCG) sets. It is run by an individual hobbyist, and you can get in touch through GitHub.

Information we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts and no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, email address or anything else about you. The checklist has no server or database of its own, so your collection and settings are never sent to us. The only thing we see is the visitor statistics described below, which can’t be linked to you.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn’t use cookies or store anything on your device, and it doesn’t identify you. It tells us things like which pages are popular, which websites sent visitors here, how quickly pages load, and the general type of device and country. We use these figures to improve the checklist, on the basis of our legitimate interests.

To count a visit, your browser loads a small script from Cloudflare at static.cloudflareinsights.com and sends these figures to cloudflareinsights.com. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details when it does. Nothing about your collection is sent.

Visits are never counted on the Shared collection and Trade list pages, or when you open a link that carries a collection, a trade code or a proposed trade, because those links hold your cards after the #. You can read how Cloudflare handles this information in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

What is stored on your device

To work, the checklist saves a small amount of information in your web browser:

  • Your collections and settings. This covers which cards you have marked in each set, how many copies you own, and display choices such as your layout and theme. It is kept in your browser’s local storage.
  • Your trades. If you use Trade, the trade you’re putting together, the last friend’s trade code you opened, the name you chose to show and your past trades are kept in local storage too.
  • Copies of the site’s files and card pictures. These let the checklist load quickly and work without an internet connection. They are kept in your browser’s cache storage, which a service worker manages. If you use the card scanner, the card recogniser (about 40 MB) and the card index for each game you scan are saved there too, so they only download once.

This information stays on your device. It isn’t sent to us or anyone else, and it isn’t used to identify or track you. Storing it is strictly necessary to give you the service you asked for, so under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and equivalent EU rules it doesn’t need your consent.

Cookies

The checklist doesn’t set any cookies, for analytics, advertising or anything else, so there is no cookie banner. We don’t use advertising, social media widgets or tracking pixels. Apart from Cloudflare’s visitor statistics script (see above), fonts and scripts are served from this website, and card pictures come from this website, TCGdex, Lorcast and our own image site (see below).

Card pictures

Most Pokémon card pictures are loaded straight from TCGdex, a free and open Pokémon card database, at assets.tcgdex.net. When your browser shows one of these pictures, it contacts TCGdex’s server, which, like any web host, receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Nothing about your collection is sent. Pictures that TCGdex doesn’t have are served from this website. Once a picture has loaded, the checklist keeps a copy on your device, so it isn’t downloaded again.

Yu-Gi-Oh! card pictures are served from img.bindertracker.co.uk, a second Binder Tracker website that is also hosted on GitHub Pages (see Hosting below). They are copies of the card scans on YGOPRODeck, made when the site is built, so your browser never contacts YGOPRODeck. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type when a picture loads. Nothing about your collection is sent, and once a picture has loaded the checklist keeps a copy on your device.

Disney Lorcana card pictures are loaded straight from Lorcast, a free Lorcana card database, at cards.lorcast.io. When your browser shows one of these pictures, it contacts Lorcast’s server, which, like any web host, receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Nothing about your collection is sent. Pictures that Lorcast doesn’t have are served from this website. Lorcast’s server doesn’t let the checklist save its pictures for offline use, so your browser may keep them in its normal cache for a while, but they aren’t saved with the rest of the site.

Camera and the card scanner

The Scan a card button uses your camera only after you open the scanner and your browser asks for, and you give, permission. Your browser shows that the camera is in use, and the scanner turns it off as soon as you close it or switch to another tab.

Recognising the card happens entirely on your device. The card recogniser is a small image-recognition model that is downloaded from this website, like the rest of the site, and runs inside your browser. Camera pictures and any photo you choose are processed in your device’s memory and then discarded: they aren’t uploaded, sent to us or anyone else, or saved. The checklist has no server that could receive them.

Scan QR code in Import works the same way: it uses the camera only while it’s open, reads the QR code on your device and never uploads or saves a picture. The QR reader (jsQR, an open-source library) is downloaded from this website like the rest of the site.

You can withdraw camera permission at any time in your browser’s site settings for bindertracker.co.uk.

Card prices, shop and social media links

Card prices are downloaded when the site is built, from tcgcsv.com, which republishes TCGplayer’s market prices. They are then served from this website along with everything else, so looking at a price doesn’t contact anyone else. The TCGplayer, eBay UK and BR Trading Post buttons in a card’s info open those websites in a new tab only when you tap them. BR Trading Post is a card shop run by the person who makes Binder Tracker, and its button searches the shop for the card’s name. Once you are on another website, its own privacy policy applies.

The TikTok, Instagram and Facebook buttons are plain links to Binder Tracker’s pages there. We don’t embed their widgets or tracking pixels, so they learn nothing about your visit unless you tap one, and then their own privacy policy applies.

Export or share, import and trade codes

When you use Export or share, the cards and copy counts in the sets you pick, your goal for each set and the name you type, if any, are encoded into the link, code or QR code itself. In a link they come after the # symbol, and browsers don’t send that part to the web server. We don’t upload or store any of it. Anyone who has the link, code or QR code can see the cards in it, so only share it with people you’re happy to show. Opening a link someone sends you shows their cards on your device, and nothing changes in your own collection unless you choose Merge or Replace.

Trade codes work the same way. Your trade code holds the cards and copy counts in every set you’re collecting (or the sets you choose), your goal for each set and the name you type, if any. A proposed trade link holds the cards in that trade and your name. Both are encoded after the #, so they aren’t sent to the web server. Anyone who scans the QR code or has the link can see what’s in it. When you open a friend’s code, it stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Files you export are saved to your own device. A file of your whole collection also holds your Portfolio value history. What happens to links, codes and files afterwards is up to you.

Hosting

The site, and the Yu-Gi-Oh! image site at img.bindertracker.co.uk, are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it in server logs for security and to run the service. We can’t see these logs. You can read how GitHub handles this information in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

The checklist is suitable for collectors of all ages. It doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

We don’t hold any personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You are in full control of the data on your device:

  • Use Reset this set in a set’s menu to clear that checklist.
  • Clear this site’s data in your browser settings to remove everything, including saved card images.

If you have a question about this policy, please contact us through GitHub. If you are in the UK, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this policy

If the checklist ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by collecting more information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
