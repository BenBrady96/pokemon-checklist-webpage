The card lists are based on the official card lists, the TCGdex and YGOPRODeck card databases and TCGplayer’s catalogue, and we work hard to keep them accurate. We can’t guarantee they are free of errors, though. For authoritative information, see the official Pokémon TCG website or the official Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG website.

Prices and pull odds

Card prices are TCGplayer market prices in US dollars, republished by tcgcsv.com and updated about once a day. The figures in pounds are converted at that day’s exchange rate. Collection values add these prices up. Prices change all the time, and the figures here are estimates only. They are not valuations, offers to buy or sell, or financial advice. Pull odds, where shown, are rough estimates based on packs opened by the community, not official figures.
