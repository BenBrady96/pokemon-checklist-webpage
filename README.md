## About

Binder Tracker is a free checklist site for trading card game collectors, with no ads, no sign-up and no tracking. Every set has its own checklist, with card images, rarities, prices and binder pages.

- **Pokémon TCG:** every English set from Base Set (1999) to Mega Evolution, including subsets, Black Star promos, Basic Energy, trainer kits, McDonald's collections, POP Series, World Championships Decks and Prize Packs.
- **Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG:** every English product since Legend of Blue Eyes White Dragon, from booster sets and reprint sets to structure decks, tins, packs and promos.
- **Disney Lorcana TCG:** every English set since The First Chapter, plus Illumineer's Quest and the numbered promo series.

Your collection is saved in your own browser, and the site works offline once you've added it to your home screen. It's a static site built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no backend.

## Features

- Standard, Master and Grand Master tiers, covering each game's extra versions: reverse holos, patterns and 1st Editions for Pokémon, every rarity for Yu-Gi-Oh!, and foils, Epic, Enchanted and Iconic cards for Lorcana
- Grid, list and binder page views, with copy counts for duplicates
- Quick add for marking lots of cards at once, with undo
- A card scanner that recognises cards through your camera, on your device
- Collection value and cost to complete, from TCGplayer market prices
- Trade codes and trade lists for comparing collections with friends
- Export or share a set, chosen sets or your whole collection, and import one, by link, code, QR code or file
- A page to view a collection someone sends you, or compare it side by side with your own
- Works offline and installs to your home screen, with light and dark themes

## Privacy

The site sets no cookies and has no advertising or tracking. Visitor numbers are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and doesn't identify anyone, and never runs on links that carry a collection or trade code. Your collection stays in your browser, and scanner photos are processed on your device and never uploaded. Card images load from TCGdex, Lorcast or Binder Tracker's own image site. Full details are in the [privacy policy](https://bindertracker.co.uk/privacy.html).
