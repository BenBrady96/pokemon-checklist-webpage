About the 30th Celebration set

Pokémon TCG: 30th Celebration marks 30 years of Pokémon. The set has 199 cards: 128 main set cards, including 30 special Pikachu Rares; 33 secret rares (18 Illustration Rares, 10 Special Illustration Rares, 2 Futuristic Rares and the 3 ultra-rare RGB Mews); 30 Classic Collection reprints of iconic cards from the game’s history; and 8 Basic Energy cards.

Choose what you’re collecting: the Standard set (the 128 main set cards), the Master set (all 199 cards) or the Grand Master set (246 cards: every card plus the 17 Black Star promos from 30th Celebration products, the 27 First Partner Illustration promos and 3 stamped or alternate prints).

This free checklist lets you tick off the cards you own, count spare copies for trading, see your progress by rarity and plan your binder pages. It works on phones and computers, even offline, and your collection stays on your device.

Help & shortcuts

Marking cards

  • Tap a card to mark it owned, and tap again to unmark it.
  • Mark many at once: on a phone, press and hold a card, then drag across others. With a mouse, click and drag. Hold near the top or bottom edge to keep scrolling.
  • Shift-click marks every card between your last click and this one.
  • Count mode () makes each tap add a copy, which is handy when logging packs. Use − to remove one.
  • Quick add takes card numbers such as 1-22, 55x2, C3, E9, RGB, P94.
  • Standard, Master or Grand Master: pick what you’re collecting above the cards. Standard is the 128 main set cards, Master is all 199 cards, and Grand Master adds the Black Star promos, the First Partner promos and stamped or alternate prints. Cards you’ve marked are kept when you switch.
  • Card info () shows where to get a card, rough pull odds and its market price, with links to check live prices.
  • Every change can be undone from the pop-up or with Ctrl+Z.

Keeping your collection safe

  • Your collection is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Use Export backup now and then, or a sync link to copy your collection to another device.
  • On iPhone and iPad, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Safari can clear saved data for websites you haven’t visited in a while, but not for apps on your Home Screen, and the app also works offline.
