About Binder Tracker

Binder Tracker is a free checklist for Pokémon TCG collectors. Every Scarlet & Violet and Mega Evolution expansion has its own page, with card images, rarities and TCGplayer prices, plus the Black Star promos and Basic Energy.

Pick what you’re collecting in each set: the Standard set (the main set), the Complete set (every card, including secret rares), the Master set (every card plus each reverse holo and Poké Ball or Master Ball pattern) or, where there are extra promos, the Grand Master set.

Your collection stays on your device, it works on phones and computers even offline, and a sync link or backup moves it to another device.
