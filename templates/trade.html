          {{names}} card trade checker

          Binder Tracker’s trade checker compares your {{tcg}} collection with a friend’s. It shows the cards each of you has that the other is missing, and checks a trade is fair with TCGplayer market prices. It’s free, with no ads, no sign-up and no tracking.

          How it works

          1. Swap trade codes. Show your code and scan your friend’s, or send it as a link. You’ll both see what each of you has that the other needs.
          2. Pick the cards. Tap Give or Get on a card to add it to the trade. You can scan a card with your camera too.
          3. Check it’s fair. The meter compares TCGplayer market prices, in pounds, dollars or euros. Condition isn’t counted, so agree the final values together.
          4. Update your collections. Update my collection changes your cards on this device. Send the trade to your friend so they can update theirs too.

          Trade FAQ

          Is the trade checker free?

          Yes. Like the rest of Binder Tracker, it’s free, with no ads, no sign-up and no tracking.

          Does my friend need Binder Tracker?

          To compare collections, yes. A trade code comes from the cards your friend has marked in Binder Tracker. Without one, you can still put a trade together from your spares and the cards you need, and check it’s fair.

          How does it work out if a trade is fair?

          It adds up the TCGplayer market price of every card on each side. Card condition isn’t counted, and cards with no market price yet are left out, so agree the final values with the person you’re trading with.

          Is my collection uploaded anywhere?

          No. Your collection and your trades stay in this browser. A trade code is a link that carries your cards from the sets you choose, and it only leaves your device when you share it.
